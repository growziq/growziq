Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Growziq offer?

Growziq offers four core services: Web Development (websites, landing pages, e-commerce), Business Automation (WhatsApp bots, CRM, workflows), UI/UX & Brand Strategy (design, identity, style guides), and AI & Cloud Solutions (AI agents, cloud hosting, API integrations).

Can you automate my business workflows?

Absolutely. We specialise in business automation from WhatsApp auto-replies and lead capture bots to full CRM integrations and order notification systems. We map your workflow and build a system that runs 24/7 for you.

How does the process work?

We start with a free discovery call to understand your goals. Then we define the scope, design and build your solution, and hand it over ready to launch. You own everything we build with no lock-ins and no hidden fees.
